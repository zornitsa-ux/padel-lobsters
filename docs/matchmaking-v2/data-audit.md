# P0.1 — Historical Data Audit

Generated by `scripts/mm-audit.mjs` (dev-only, committed; re-run with
`node scripts/mm-audit.mjs` against a running local stack — `npm run db:start`)
for the `History.jsx` numbers, plus **read-only SELECTs against production**
(via the Supabase MCP server, `mcp__supabase__execute_sql`) for the DB-era
numbers, since the local stack only holds seed data (see §1). Produced
2026-07-09/10.

This answers the three P0.1 acceptance questions for DESIGN §6 calibration
(fitting `D`, `beta`, `G`, `N_ref` and a Brier-score backtest):

1. **How many matches have scores?** 290 total — 116 from `History.jsx` +
   174 from production's `matches` table (4 completed tournaments).
2. **What is the median total points per match?** 7, consistently across
   both sources — see the N_ref discrepancy flagged in §3.
3. **Which historical players are unlinked?** 16 of the 68 distinct
   `History.jsx` names have no `player_aliases` row in production at all
   (46 are linked, 6 are explicitly skipped) — see §5.

## 0. Data sources

- **History.jsx** — `TOURNAMENTS` constant, actually defined in
  `src/data/historicalTournaments.js` and consumed by
  `src/features/history/History.jsx` (the brief said `src/components/History.jsx`;

  directly rather than hand-copying data.

  `public.player_aliases`. **Queried against production** (project
  `enjhugmqjtfakwivpmvf`), read-only `SELECT`s only, no writes — the local
  stack has no real match data to query (§1). The committed script,
  `scripts/mm-audit.mjs`, is intentionally left pointed at the **local**
  stack only (it's a dev tool that shouldn't embed prod credentials or
  require prod access to run); the numbers in this doc for the DB era come
  from ad hoc production queries run alongside the script, not from the
  script's own output.
- **Alias/linking mechanism** — `public.player_aliases (historical_name text
primary key, player_id uuid, skipped boolean)`, read by
  `src/lib/ratingsRecompute.js` to resolve `History.jsx` names to real player
  rows. (There is a second, unrelated `lobster_name_aliases` map in
  `localStorage`, written by `src/features/history/aliasStorage.js` — that one
  only fixes display-name collisions on the History page and has no bearing on
  rating/matchmaking data; it is not a data-linking mechanism and is not used
  in this audit.)

## 3. Point-total distribution per match (→ N_ref)

All matches with usable scores, `s1 + s2` (exact, computed from raw rows —
not just per-tournament Postgres percentile aggregates):

| Source        | n       | min   | median | mean     | p90   | max    |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- | ------ | -------- | ----- | ------ |
| History.jsx   | 116     | 3     | **7**  | 6.99     | 9     | 14     |
| Production DB | 174     | 3     | **7**  | 6.44     | 8     | 10     |
| **Combined**  | **290** | **3** | **7**  | **6.66** | **8** | **14** |

Per production tournament:

| name              | n   | min | median | mean | p90 | max |
| ----------------- | --- | --- | ------ | ---- | --- | --- |
| LOBStournament #5 | 48  | 3   | 6      | 6.00 | 7   | 8   |
| LOBStournament #6 | 48  | 4   | 6      | 6.27 | 8   | 9   |
| LOBStournament #7 | 42  | 4   | 7      | 6.88 | 8   | 10  |
| LOBStournament #8 | 36  | 4   | 7      | 6.75 | 8   | 9   |

**Flag for the coordinator — real N_ref mismatch, now confirmed on 290
matches across two independent sources.** DESIGN §4.1 expects `N_ref`
(median points of a full-length match) "≈ 24". The measured median `s1+s2`
per match is **7**, consistently, in both `History.jsx` (n=116) and
production (n=174) — off by more than 3× and not an artifact of thin or
hand-copied data. Digging into why: `History.jsx`'s `players[].total` field
(a player's cumulative score across an entire event) has a much closer
median:

| id      | rounds | n players | median standings total |
| ------- | ------ | --------- | ---------------------- |
| apr2026 | 4      | 20        | 14.5                   |
| mar2026 | 6      | 32        | 22                     |
| jan2026 | 6      | 32        | 22                     |
| dec2025 | 6      | 32        | 21                     |

For the three 6-round events, the median **cumulative per-player total across
the whole tournament** is 21–22 — much closer to DESIGN's "≈ 24" than the
per-match total of 7. This strongly suggests the "≈ 24" figure in DESIGN
§4.1 was anchored on a player's _tournament-total_ score rather than the
_single-match_ `s1+s2` the formula actually uses. This is a data observation
only — no calibration math was performed here (out of scope for P0.1) — but
the coordinator should resolve this before M1.6 fits `N_ref`, since a 3×
error in `N_ref` changes every match's reliability weight `w`.

## 6. Verdict: is history thick enough for §6 calibration?

**Yes for volume, not yet for correctness.** Combined, `History.jsx` and
production give **290 real scored matches** across 8 events (4 fully-recorded
hardcoded events + 4 completed production tournaments), with consistent,
low-variance point totals (median 7, range 3–14) across every event and both
sources — enough rows and enough spread to attempt a real `D`/`beta`/`G` fit
and a Brier-score backtest against Glicko-2. This is a materially better
picture than the local-only view, which showed zero DB-era matches and would
have wrongly suggested falling back to hand-set constants.

Two things should be resolved before M1.6 proceeds, though:

1. **`N_ref` as written in DESIGN §4.1 looks miscalibrated** — measured
   median is 7, not ≈24, confirmed identically on both `History.jsx` (n=116)
   and production (n=174) (§3). The tournament-total figures (median 21–22)
   line up much better with "≈24," suggesting a units mix-up in the design
   assumption. This needs a coordinator decision before fitting `N_ref`,
   since it changes every match's reliability weight.
2. **16 of 68 historical names (24%) are unlinked** in production (§5) — real
   coverage, not a seed artifact, and directly reduces the usable match count
   from `History.jsx` per `ratingsRecompute.js`'s all-4-players-must-resolve
   rule. Worth an admin pass to link or skip these before the calibration
   replay, especially the `apr2026` ladies names.

Net: PLAN.md §6's risk ("thin calibration data → fallback to hand-set
constants") does **not** apply here — 290 matches across 8 real events is a
reasonable base for a real fit. The gate should be the two data-quality
issues above, not data volume.
